# A-CHKO
### Watch. Connect. Discover Opportunities.

A-CHKO is a modern social networking platform and opportunity discovery hub designed to connect people, creators, students, entrepreneurs, professionals, and communities worldwide.

The platform aims to combine social networking, short-form content, educational resources, career opportunities, business networking, and personal development in one accessible digital ecosystem.

**Project Creator:** mumi

**Platform:** Web application / Progressive Web App (PWA) roadmap

**Primary Audience:** Students, creators, entrepreneurs, job seekers, professionals, and young people worldwide.

---

## 1. Vision

Our vision is to build a global digital platform where people can:

- Connect with individuals who share their interests.
- Discover scholarships, jobs, internships, grants, and competitions.
- Share knowledge, creativity, and experiences.
- Build communities around common goals.
- Discover business ideas and entrepreneurship opportunities.
- Showcase talents, projects, and professional skills.
- Develop meaningful personal and professional connections.

A-CHKO aims to bring social connection and opportunity discovery together in one platform.

---

## 2. Mission

Our mission is to make opportunities easier to discover and connections easier to build.

A-CHKO aims to help users move beyond consuming content by encouraging them to learn, create, participate, collaborate, and grow.

---

## 3. Core Features

### 3.1 Home Feed

The home feed is the central place where users discover content from the community.

Planned features include:

- Text posts.
- Images and videos.
- Educational content.
- Business ideas.
- Career advice.
- Entertainment content.
- Community updates.
- Recommended content.
- Like, comment, save, and share actions.
- Content categories and search.

### 3.2 A-CHKO Shorts

A short-form content experience for sharing quick videos and creative ideas.

Planned features include:

- Uploading short videos.
- Watching videos.
- Likes and comments.
- Sharing and saving videos.
- Following creators.
- Hashtags.
- Music and sound integration.
- Replies and reposts.
- Remix functionality.
- Reporting inappropriate content.
- Content discovery based on interests.

Video uploads, playback, music, and advanced interactions require appropriate backend and media-storage integrations.
